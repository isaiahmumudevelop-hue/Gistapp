Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account we collect: your email address, display name, and optionally a profile photo, cover photo, and bio. We do not collect your phone number, location, or contacts.

2. How we use your data

Your email is used only to log you in and send account notifications (like verification links). Your messages are stored in our database so you can access them from any device.

3. What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not track you across other websites.

4. Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from Settings, which removes all your data from our servers. You can also export your data.

5. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Messages transit over encrypted HTTPS. Two-step verification is available for extra protection.

6. Contact

Questions? Email support@gistapp.app
